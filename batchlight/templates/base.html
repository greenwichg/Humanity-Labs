Humanity Labs

Free tools to check medicines and food against official safety alerts. Medicine alerts are powered by Batchlight.

Important

We relay official alerts and published test results, with a link to every source. We do not test products ourselves and cannot tell whether a product is adulterated. This is not medical advice.

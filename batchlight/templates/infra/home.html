What this covers today

Today the passports come from: every ongoing central project of ₹150 crore or more (MoSPI's monthly report), PMGSY rural roads (India Data Portal's copy of the Ministry of Rural Development's data), World Bank–financed programmes with their civil-works tenders and contract awards, and audit reports from the Comptroller and Auditor General. State roads and buildings, city drains and smaller central works are not connected yet, so many public works are not here. See every source and what is missing.

How to read a Project Passport

  • Every answer links to the official record it comes from, and is labelled: official record, official finding, calculated by us, news report, or citizen evidence.
  • When a record is not published, the passport says “Not published” and why. We never fill gaps with guesses.
  • A contract award, a passed deadline or a cost change is not, by itself, evidence of wrongdoing.

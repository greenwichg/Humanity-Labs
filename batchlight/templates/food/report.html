Make an official complaint too

Only regulators can investigate and take legal action. In India:

  • Complain on FSSAI's Food Safety Connect website or in the Food Safety Connect app; if you complain online you get a ticket number by SMS to track it. FSSAI also lists the toll-free helpline 1800-11-2100 and the email helpdesk-foscos@fssai.gov.in.
  • Contact your state's Commissioner of Food Safety or local food safety officer.
  • For a consumer dispute with a seller, call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915 (8 am to 8 pm) or 1800114000, WhatsApp +91 88000 01915, or use consumerhelpline.gov.in.
  • You can also pay to have the food analysed by a government Food Analyst yourself (Section 40 of the Food Safety and Standards Act): see Get food tested.

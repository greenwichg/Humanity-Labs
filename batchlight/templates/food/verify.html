What a packaged food label in India should show

Under FSSAI's labelling rules, packaged food generally carries the items below. Some very small packs are exempt from parts of this. Missing details are worth reporting.

  • ☐ Name of the food
  • ☐ List of ingredients
  • ☐ Nutrition information
  • ☐ Vegetarian (green) or non-vegetarian (brown) symbol
  • ☐ Food additives declared
  • ☐ Name and full address of the maker or packer
  • ☐ FSSAI logo and 14-digit licence number
  • ☐ Net quantity
  • ☐ Batch, lot or code number
  • ☐ Date of manufacture or packing
  • ☐ Best before or use by date
  • ☐ Country of origin, for imported food

Source: Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020, published by FSSAI.

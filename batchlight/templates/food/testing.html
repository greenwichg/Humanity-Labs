Get food tested

Only a properly equipped laboratory can confirm what is in food. Here is how to use one, what it costs, and labs whose accreditation a curator has checked.

How to get a sample tested in India

  1. Choose an accredited lab. Look for a lab accredited by NABL for the test you need, and preferably notified by FSSAI. Check its certificate number on NABL's website.
  2. Ask for a specific test. "Is it adulterated?" can't be tested. Ask for named tests: for example pesticide residues, lead, aflatoxin, Salmonella, or a named adulterant.
  3. Keep the sample sealed. Use an unopened pack where possible, and note the batch number, barcode and where you bought it.
  4. Read the report against the legal limit. A result is only meaningful compared with the limit for that food.

The Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (Section 40) also lets a buyer have food analysed by a food analyst for a fee. Ask your state food safety department how to do this.

What happens next

  1. The photo is checked for blur, darkness and duplicates. A photo already on the site is not posted twice.
  2. Faces and number plates are blurred.
  3. Computer vision describes what is visible and scores its condition, with its evidence and confidence.
  4. The area's MP and MLA are listed, and a reference number is checked against official records.
  5. The post goes public. Others can add notes and discuss it.

A photo works best when

  • it is taken in daylight and in focus;
  • the damage, or the good condition, fills much of the frame;
  • it shows one thing: one stretch of road, one bridge, one building.

A score describes only what is visible. It never says whether a structure is safe.

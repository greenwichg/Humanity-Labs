Why this matters

In 2025-26, State and UT drug controllers reported testing about 1.4 lakh medicine samples in India: 3,012 were declared Not of Standard Quality and 283 spurious or adulterated (Health Ministry answer in Parliament). Inspectors pick these samples during inspections and market checks, so the numbers do not show what share of all medicines is bad. Contaminated cough syrups killed children in several countries between 2022 and 2025. Alerts exist, but they rarely reach the pharmacy shelf or the family medicine cabinet in time.

For pharmacies, clinics and families

  1. 1. List what you hold. Add items by hand or import a spreadsheet, with batch numbers.
  2. 2. Get matched. When an alert from the US FDA, UK MHRA, Health Canada or WHO, or an Indian alert added by our team, names one of your batches, you're told, with the evidence. Not included yet: India's CDSCO monthly drug alerts and state drug controllers' notices.
  3. 3. Act and record. Quarantine or return the stock and record it, so your team and inspectors can see what was done.

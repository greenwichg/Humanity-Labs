Every finding links to the regulator, study or report it comes from, and says which it is. We never say a product is adulterated unless a regulator or an accredited laboratory has found it, and we say so when we know this only from a news report.

What we do

  • Collect medicine and food recalls from the US FDA, UK MHRA, Health Canada, the Canadian Food Inspection Agency and the WHO. A few Indian food and medicine actions are added by hand, and each one says whether it comes from an official notice, an official lab result or a news report. India's CDSCO monthly drug alerts are not included yet.
  • Summarise what official tests, peer-reviewed studies and news reports found, with each source labelled and linked.
  • Help you report a problem and reach the right authority.

What we don't do

  • We don't test products, and no app or photo can prove adulteration.
  • "No alert found" does not prove a product is genuine or safe.
  • We don't give medical advice. If an alert's notice says not to use a product, follow it, and see a doctor if someone has taken it. Otherwise, don't stop a medicine you need without asking a pharmacist or doctor.

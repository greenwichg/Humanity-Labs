Every finding links to the regulator, study or report it comes from. We never say a product is adulterated unless an accredited laboratory or a regulator has found it.

What we do

  • Collect medicine and food recalls from the US FDA, UK MHRA, Health Canada, the Canadian Food Inspection Agency and the WHO, and add notices from India's FSSAI by hand.
  • Summarise what official surveys and peer-reviewed studies found, with links.
  • Help you report a problem and reach the right authority.

What we don't do

  • We don't test products, and no app or photo can prove adulteration.
  • "No alert found" does not prove a product is genuine or safe.
  • We don't give medical advice. Don't stop a medicine without asking a pharmacist or doctor.

How Humanity Labs works

Regulators and scientists regularly find medicines and food that are contaminated, mislabelled, sub-standard or fake. Their findings are scattered across websites, PDFs and press releases, and rarely reach the people holding the product. Humanity Labs gathers them, links every one to its source, and makes them easy to check.

Medicines: Batchlight

Batchlight reads medicine and medical device alerts from the US FDA, the UK MHRA, Health Canada and the WHO, and curators add alerts from regulators that publish only PDFs. Batch numbers are extracted from each alert, so anyone can check the batch printed on their strip or box. Pharmacies, clinics and distributors can list their stock and be told when a regulator flags a batch they hold. A batch number alone is never enough for a match: the product name, manufacturer or product code must agree too, and every match shows its reasons.

Food

The Food Safety module collects food recalls from the US FDA and the Canadian Food Inspection Agency automatically. India's FSSAI does not yet publish recalls as open data, so curators add its notices by hand from official statements and reliable reports, with links. You can search recalls by product, brand, batch code or barcode; read what official surveys and peer-reviewed studies found in everyday foods; check a pack's FSSAI number, barcode and label; report a suspicious product; and find out how to get food tested properly.

Our evidence rules

  • Every alert and finding links to its source. Findings show whether they are official data, a peer-reviewed study or a news report. Alerts added by our curators say so, and say whether their source is the regulator's notice or a published report.
  • We never say a product is adulterated unless a regulator or an accredited laboratory has found it.
  • We don't offer home tests, photo checks or AI that claims to detect adulteration, because none has been shown to work reliably.
  • Reports from the public are reviewed by a curator and never published as findings.

What we are not

We are not a regulator or a laboratory, and this is not medical advice. "No alert found" does not prove that a product is genuine or safe. Don't stop taking a medicine without speaking to a pharmacist or doctor.

Privacy

We store products and batch numbers, not patients. Food reports ask only for what is needed to follow up, and your connection's address is kept only as a one-way code to limit spam. We never sell data.

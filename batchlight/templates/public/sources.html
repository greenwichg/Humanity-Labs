Data sources

Humanity Labs reads public alerts from the sources below, mostly from January 2024 onward. Automated sources are refreshed on a schedule. Our team also adds a few Indian alerts by hand; each one says whether it comes from an official notice, an official lab result or a news report.

Not covered yet: India's CDSCO monthly drug alerts, state drug controllers' notices and most FSSAI actions.

India's drug regulator, CDSCO, publishes monthly lists of medicine samples that government laboratories found not of standard quality, spurious or adulterated, on cdsco.gov.in under “Drug Alert”. These lists are not included in our searches yet.

Licences

openFDA data is in the public domain. GOV.UK content is published under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Health Canada and Canadian Food Inspection Agency data are published under the Open Government Licence – Canada. WHO alerts are summarised with a link to the original notice on who.int. Alerts added by our team link to the regulator's own notice or test result when we can open it, otherwise to a news report, and are labelled accordingly.

Why this matters

In low- and middle-income countries, roughly one medical product in ten is substandard or falsified. Contaminated cough syrups killed children in several countries between 2022 and 2025. Alerts exist, but they rarely reach the pharmacy shelf or the family medicine cabinet in time.

For pharmacies, clinics and families

  1. 1. List what you hold. Add items by hand or import a spreadsheet, with batch numbers.
  2. 2. Get matched. When any regulator flags one of your batches, you're told, with the evidence.
  3. 3. Act and record. Quarantine or return the stock and record it, so your team and inspectors can see what was done.
